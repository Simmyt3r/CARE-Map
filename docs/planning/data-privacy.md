# Data Privacy & Retention

**Status:** Draft — reflects the team's working decision from 2026-09-01; recommend legal review before treating as final
**Last updated:** 2026-09-01

CARE-Map collects personal data from two groups: registered community users (name, contact info) and anyone submitting a public report (photos, and optionally contact info if they choose to register). This falls under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA), which established the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) as the enforcing authority.

## Guiding Principles (from NDPA)

- **Lawfulness, fairness, transparency** — data is processed only for CARE-Map's stated purpose: tracking interventions and community reports.
- **Purpose limitation & data minimization** — only the fields needed to operate reporting and tracking are collected.
- **Storage limitation** — data isn't kept longer than necessary (see retention rules below).
- **Consent** — registration and report submission require explicit, informed consent, in plain language, before any personal data is collected.

## Retention Rules

**Resolved 2026-09-01:**

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Report content (description, photos, location) and any personal info attached to it | Kept for 2 years after the report is marked resolved, then anonymized — personal identifiers are stripped, but the underlying tracking data may be kept for historical/trend analysis. |
| Registered community accounts | Kept while active. Deletable by the user at any time from within the app. |
| Staff/admin accounts | Governed by standard ACReSAL staff data-handling procedures; out of scope for this document. |

## Data Subject Rights

Consistent with the NDPA, registered users can:
- Request a copy of the personal data held about them
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Delete their account and associated personal data at any time

## Open Items for Legal Review

- [ ] Confirm whether CARE-Map's scale (14 LGAs, open public reporting) makes it a data controller "of major importance" under the NDPA, which would trigger NDPC registration and a Data Protection Officer requirement.
- [ ] Check the 2-year retention window against any ACReSAL-specific record-keeping obligations (e.g. World Bank reporting requirements) that might call for longer retention of de-identified data.
- [ ] Define the breach-notification process — the NDPA requires notifying the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach, and affected users promptly where there's high risk to them.

This reflects the project team's working decision, not formal legal advice. A qualified Nigerian data protection lawyer should review it before public launch, given the platform's government affiliation and public-facing data collection.
